## 2026-10-16

- The weekly reading built: a procedure in plain words (docs/weekly-reading.md, adapted from The Belt Equation's scan plan and procedure, every design choice with its confidence and an alternative, the schedule proposed), the reading record's layout in the record layout document (one file per run under data/readings/, never edited, four verdicts: quiet, noted, stage change, flagged) with the export script refusing any record that breaks it, and an optional list of extra search terms (`watch`) on each upgrade. The scanner may move a stage one rung on a record of the kind the upgrade names, and may not touch a test, a settler, a verify list or the headline rung; everything else is a flagged item for John.

The second: the Australian regulator's page the psychedelic entry could not load on 2026-10-16 loaded tonight and matches the entry, so that claim can be cleared. Two claims still to check were looked at and not settled (the first mitochondrial-donation child's exact birth date; whether the 2016 Pittsburgh touch paper itself includes the robotic hand), and a third was found supported but unread (Vidal's 1977 maze-by-brain-signal paper as an earlier first human for the scalp headset).
